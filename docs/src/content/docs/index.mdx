## Eight lines and a port

No database to provision and no build step. Bring your own models, whether that is Ollama on the same
machine or an API key you already have.

    image: ghcr.io/cedhuf/llooma:latest
    restart: unless-stopped
    ports:
      - '4173:4173'
    environment:
      - VITE_ALLOWED_HOSTS=localhost

## When local isn't an option

Running Ollama on your own machine means no third party at all, and that's what I'd recommend
first. It needs a decent GPU though, and no phone has one. If that's not realistic, someone else
runs the model, and it's worth caring who.

[Infomaniak](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/hosting/ai-tools) is the one I like: Swiss datacentres
they own and run, open-weight models, prompts they don't log or train on, and the GPU heat goes
into warming homes nearby. Any other OpenAI-compatible provider works just as well here.

</section>
