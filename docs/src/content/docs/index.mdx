## When local isn't an option

Ollama on your own machine is this app with no third party in it at all, and it is the version worth
reaching for first. It also needs hardware, which not everyone has and no phone does. The moment it
is out of reach the question stops being technical: someone else runs the model, so who?

Infomaniak is a preset here for that reason rather than by accident. What they say about it, on
[their own page](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/hosting/ai-tools):

- **It runs in Switzerland**, in datacentres they own, with no dependency on operators elsewhere.
  Swiss rather than EU, which is a real distinction, though they commit to the GDPR as well as the
  Swiss FADP.
- **The models are open weights**, so what you build on does not turn on one vendor's roadmap.
- **Prompts are not logged**, not used for training, and not used to improve the service.
- **The heat is not thrown away.** The power is renewable and fully recovered: 6,000 homes warmed in
  winter, the equivalent of 20,000 showers a day in summer. The GPUs run without dedicated air
  conditioning, on adiabatic cooling, and draw water only in a heatwave.

That last one is the reason it gets a paragraph. The energy cost of this technology is its least
answered question, and heating a neighbourhood with the waste is a better answer than most.
